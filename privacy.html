Privacy policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

What we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no contact forms that transmit your details to us. Everything you type into a calculator stays in your browser and is never sent to a server.

Advertising

This site displays advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting aggregate analytics to count page views. No individual visitor is identified.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase. Requests about advertising data should be directed to the advertising provider.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the publisher, Legacy Bridge Alliance Group.
