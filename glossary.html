Glossary

The terms that appear on quotes and disclosures, in plain English.

Advertisement
TermWhat it means
APRAnnual Percentage Rate. The interest rate plus lender fees and points, expressed as a yearly figure. The right number for comparing offers.
AmortisationHow each payment splits between interest and principal. Early payments are mostly interest; the balance shifts over the life of the loan.
Discount pointA fee of 1% of the loan paid up front to lower the rate. Worth it only if you keep the loan past the break-even period.
Origination feeWhat the lender charges to process the loan. Included in APR.
EscrowAn account the servicer uses to hold and pay your property taxes and insurance.
PMIPrivate mortgage insurance. Usually required below 20% down; protects the lender, not you.
LTVLoan-to-value. Loan amount divided by property value. Lower LTV generally prices better.
DTIDebt-to-income. Monthly debt payments divided by gross monthly income.
Rate lockA lender's commitment to hold a quoted rate for a set period while the loan closes.
Loan EstimateThe standardised three-page disclosure lenders must provide within three business days of a mortgage application.
Prime rateThe rate banks charge their most creditworthy commercial customers. A base for many variable consumer products.
Federal funds rateThe Federal Reserve's policy rate for overnight interbank lending. Influences everything else indirectly.
Secured / unsecuredWhether the loan is backed by collateral. Secured lending prices lower because the lender can recover an asset.
UnderwritingThe lender's assessment of whether, and on what terms, to lend.
